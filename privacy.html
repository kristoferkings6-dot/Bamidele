Kringstar Atlas Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Kringstar Atlas collects the information you submit through this buyer assessment to understand your property preferences, assess whether an advisor conversation is appropriate, contact you about your request, and route qualified requests to the relevant property advisor.

Information we collect

This may include your name, WhatsApp phone number, optional email, location, property preference, budget, initial-payment range, funding method, buying reason, consent choices, campaign information, and submission time.

How we use it

We use the information to assess buyer readiness, provide general home-buying information, contact you about suitable property options, and improve our advertising and lead qualification process. Buyers who do not meet the direct-release requirements are not given the advisor handoff.

Sharing

Where you consent and meet the release requirements, your request may be shared with the relevant property advisor so they can respond to your inquiry. We do not sell your submitted details as a standalone contact list.

Your choices

You may ask Kringstar Atlas to stop contacting you or to explain how your submitted information is being used. Contact the Kringstar Atlas representative who provided this assessment link and include the phone number or lead reference used in your submission.
