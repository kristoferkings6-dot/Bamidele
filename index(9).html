05 / Questions worth asking

No fog. Just the terms.

Do you guarantee property sales?

No. We build and optimize the acquisition system. Sales also depend on property quality, pricing, follow-up, inspections, negotiations, and closing.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The Kringstar Atlas management fee is separate from the advertising budget agreed before launch.

What is a qualified buyer opportunity?

A prospect who provides valid details and useful context such as preferred location, property type, realistic budget, funding position, and purchase timeline.
